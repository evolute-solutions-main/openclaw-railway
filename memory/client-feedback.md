# Client Feedback Log

This file captures client feedback about lead quality, communication, objections, pricing, fit, and other insights that inform campaign improvements.

## Format
Each entry should include:
- **Date** — when feedback was received
- **Client** — which client provided it
- **Topic** — category (lead quality, communication, objections, pricing, fit, etc.)
- **Feedback** — what they said (exact quote preferred)
- **Action/Note** — any follow-up needed

## Entries

### Example
**Date:** [date]
**Client:** [client]
**Topic:** Lead Quality
**Feedback:** "The leads from Google Ads are booking appointments, but most are just price shopping."
**Action/Note:** Discuss with Evolute team how to pre-qualify for seriousness/budget before booking.
