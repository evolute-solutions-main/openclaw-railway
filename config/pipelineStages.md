## Rule
If feedback from client implies a stage change, make the update immediately. Don't wait to be asked.

## Pipeline Stages
1. Lead Captured
Lead inputted their info on one of our ads, but hasn't replied to our outreach yet.
2. Replied (no appt scheduled)
Lead replied to our outreach, but hasn't scheduled an appointment yet.
3. Follow Up (no appt scheduled)
Lead replied, hasn't scheduled an appointment yet, but needs to be followed up with at some point in the future
4. Booked Consultation
Lead is scheduled for an upcoming appointment.
5. Reschedule
Lead had appointment scheduled at one point, but it was cancelled/postponed and needs to be rescheduled.
6. **Consultation DONE->Organize it!**
Leads go here after their appointment time is in the past. This is a queue that indicates we need client feedback about what happened at the appointment. You need to understand the outcome of the meeting (ex: if the estimate was made, sent, accepted, rejected). If you learn the estimate was sent, you should move the lead out of this column, and into one of the columns used for after estimates were sent.
7. Sent Estimate: Waiting Response
The client met with the lead, and sent them a proposal, and are waiting for a final decision. They should make sure to follow up with these people.
8. Closed Deal!
Lead accepted proposal.
9. Sent Estimate: Rejected
Lead denied proposal.
10. No Response After Automated Follow Up
Lead did not respond to 1 week of follow up messages. Paused outreach for now, and put into this column.
11. Not Interested
People who are not interested/unqualified.
